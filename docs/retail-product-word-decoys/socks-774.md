# Socks (gen#774)

A plain match for `socks` used to fill its top 24 with real socks, so the demo showed nothing
between interpretation off and on. One mention in a title could not reach them: a boot sock's `text`
says the word three times (its title and two category nodes) and is short. Six rows, `PLT-DY7740` to
`PLT-DY7745` in `feature_decoys/socks_774.py`, are real products a shop names for socks: Moisturising
Gel Socks and Exfoliating Foot Peel Socks (Foot Creams), a Mesh Wash Bag for Socks (Wash Bags), a
Socks and Underwear Drawer Organiser (Garment Storage), a Socks Puppet Craft Kit (Craft Kits) and
Cable Pulling Socks (Cables). Every row but the cable grip says the word three times in a short
description, which is what reaches the top; the cable grip says it once. The seventh decoy, Absorbent
Spill Socks, is the industrial type `spill_socks` in the new Safety > Spill Control > Spill Absorbents
leaf, in `product_types/safety/` because a module of `decoys/` serves a seed. It reaches the Combined
index with the next industrial release the customization pins; the six retail rows do not wait for it.

Real socks gain the other half: their titles say how they are made, so a short title no longer wins
on length alone. `sock_titles.py` holds the detail the generated socks add; the authored rows carry
theirs in their own title. An apparel decoy of class 1, 2, 6 or 7, or a men's decoy of class 6 or 7, stays short, because its class holds
it to a shape (a named part last, a real compound, a short brand title).

Each decoy sits under no Socks node and declares the category policy, which drops them all when
interpretation is on. `tests/test_retail_socks_decoys_774.py` holds the ranking, computed from the
`_explain` figures of the loaded index, and the rule.
