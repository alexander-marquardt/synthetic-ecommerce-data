# Electronics (gen#332)

Five device words meet products of other departments, in
`feature_decoys/electronics.py` (15 rows: DY701 to DY705, DY708, DY711, DY714, DY719 to DY725): `mouse` (a cushion, a throw, a basket),
`tablet` (dishwasher tablets, Scottish tablet), `monitor` (a lizard cushion, throw and basket),
`mobile` (a coat stand, a clothes rack, a basket on wheels) and `charger` (three charger plates).
Each row carries `device_type`, the Electronics type whose word the title holds, and the department
policy drops it. `apple` is a fruit word since gen#720: its juice, candle and tree (DY716 to DY718)
are ordinary products and left this table. See
[retail-electronics.md](../retail-electronics.md).
