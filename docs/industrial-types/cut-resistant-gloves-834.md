# Cut-resistant gloves: a type built from model lines (gen#834)

`cut_resistant_gloves` (`Safety > Hand Protection > Cut-Resistant Gloves`) is a modelled type, deepened from its one-SKU breadth row. ANSI/ISEA 105 rates a glove's cut resistance and says nothing of its sizes, so the type has no size table: it is the 29 model lines of a distributor's cut-resistant glove guide, each one cut level (`ANSI Cut Level`, A2 to A8), shell yarn (`Material`), knit gauge (`Knit Gauge`) and palm coating (`Coating`). A product is one brand's glove of one model line, and its SKUs are a run of neighbouring EN ISO 21420 hand sizes, `X-Small` to `XX-Large`. The module's docstring names the guide, the day it was read, the guide's item numbers behind each line and what was left out (sleeves, mesh, jersey and leather gloves; color, which the guide does not give). The price is the census's 22 gloves (`price.DEEPENED_CENSUS`), every size of one glove at one price; the cut-level premium is a declared estimate. `tests/test_type_cut_resistant_gloves.py` runs the per-type gates. Its picture is the pair it is sold as (gen#1217): the back of one knit glove behind the palm of the other, the palm in its coating's usual color (the guide states none; `Uncoated` is bare knit and `PVC Dots` is dotted), each with a highlight and a shadow, at the proportions of its hand size and fitted to the sheet by its own turned outline. `tests/test_fill_safety_hand_1217.py` holds every size at each of the 24 angles to 50% of the product area, or to the floor it names for an angle: 39% to 49% from 45 to 150 degrees, where no arrangement tried of a nearly square pair reaches half at every size (a solid square at 45 degrees covers 41%).
