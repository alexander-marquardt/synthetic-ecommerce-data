# Cut-resistant gloves: a type built from model lines (gen#834)

`cut_resistant_gloves` (`Safety > Hand Protection > Cut-Resistant Gloves`) is a modelled type, deepened from its one-SKU breadth row. ANSI/ISEA 105 rates a glove's cut resistance and says nothing of its sizes, so the type has no size table: it is the 29 model lines of a distributor's cut-resistant glove guide, each one cut level (`ANSI Cut Level`, A2 to A8), shell yarn (`Material`), knit gauge (`Knit Gauge`) and palm coating (`Coating`). A product is one brand's glove of one model line, and its SKUs are a run of neighbouring EN ISO 21420 hand sizes, `X-Small` to `XX-Large`. The module's docstring names the guide, the day it was read, the guide's item numbers behind each line and what was left out (sleeves, mesh, jersey and leather gloves; color, which the guide does not give). The price is the census's 22 gloves (`price.DEEPENED_CENSUS`), every size of one glove at one price; the cut-level premium is a declared estimate. `tests/test_type_cut_resistant_gloves.py` runs the per-type gates.
