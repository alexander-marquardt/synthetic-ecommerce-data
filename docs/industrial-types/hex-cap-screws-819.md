# Hex head cap screws (#819)

`screw_hex_head` is the fifth cap screw, in `Cap Screws/Hex Head Cap Screws`
beside the four hexagon socket styles. It is turned by its head, not a key.

It is not the hex bolt (`bolt_hex`, under `Bolts`): it has a washer face under
the head and is made to be tightened into a tapped hole, and catalogs sell the
two as separate lines.

- Inch only, ASME B18.2.1: 1/4-20 to 1-8 UNC and the UNF size of each, 1/2" to
  6" long.
- SAE J429 Grade 2, 5 and 8 (`Material` `Grade 2 Steel`, `Grade 5 Steel`,
  `Grade 8 Alloy Steel`) in `Plain`, `Zinc Plated` or `Zinc Yellow`.
- The head, washer face, thread length and lengths are the library table
  `asme_b18_2_1_hex_cap_screws_in`; the drawing stands the hex on its washer
  face.
- The must-stock list carries the product the demo query names: `7/16-14`,
  `1-3/4"`, `Grade 8 Alloy Steel`, `Zinc Yellow`. The length wheel is kept per
  size and per feed so that entry is reachable (the size, pair and run offset
  would otherwise lock in step).
