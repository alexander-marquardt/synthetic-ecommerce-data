# Bags: crossbody, shoulder, clutch, belt, messenger and laptop bags (gen#1114)

A shopper who searched for a crossbody bag, a shoulder bag or a clutch found five Handbag styles
(`bag_style` Crossbody, Shoulder or Clutch, gen#207). Each is now a type with a leaf of its own
under `Womens > Accessories > Bags`. The five Handbags stay Handbags. The belt bag has a leaf of its
own there too (the five Waist Bags are decoys and stay where they are), and the men's messenger bag
and laptop bag open `Mens > Accessories > Bags`. A Laptop Sleeve stays in Electronics.

| Type | Spec | Leaf | Forms (`bag_shape`) | Materials | SKU axis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crossbody Bag | `type_specs/crossbody_bag.py` | `Crossbody Bags` | Saddle, Camera, Satchel | Leather, Faux Leather, Canvas, Nylon | color x size (Small, Medium) |

All six are derivable and offer the six main colors (gen#275). The shared pieces (the path, the
brands, the colorways, `bag_row()` and `decoy()`) are in `bags_common.py`, and the range names are
in `line_grammar_families/bags.py` and `bags_carry.py` (the last three types, a family of their own so
the first three keep their range names). The SKUs each type holds are in
[the count table](../catalogue-mix-837.md#counts-added-by-the-gap-issues).

**What a derived style may change.** Only the material, so a description names no finish that only
one material has (no "smooth", no "wipe-clean"). The form, the closure and a crossbody's, a belt
bag's or a messenger's strap are frozen (`scale_text.TYPE_FROZEN_KEYS`): the text and the drawing follow them, and a saddle bag
closes with a flap.

**Drawings.** `kit_bags.py` draws `crossbody_bag`, `shoulder_bag` and `clutch_bag`;
`kit_bags_carry.py` draws `belt_bag`, `messenger_bag` and `laptop_bag`. `bag_shape`
picks the form and `material` the surface: a weave, ripstop lines, a nap, a sheen, a pile, a pebble
or a topstitched edge. Both kits are in the accessories group of the redraw order, gen#1198
(`scripts/redraw_order_1129.py`).

**Decoys.** Each type has a class 1, a class 4 and a class 6 decoy. "Crossbody Bag with Red Strap"
is a black bag whose strap is drawn red (`details.register_parts`), and so are "Belt Bag with Red
Strap" and "Messenger Bag with Red Strap". The red lining of the shoulder
bag, the clutch and the laptop bag is inside a closed bag, where no picture shows it, so only the
description names it. The class-4 decoys name a material in the title that the bag is not made of: "Faux
Leather Saddle Crossbody Bag" (`Faux Leather` is the one name, so no faux leather bag is titled
Leather-Look), "Suede-Look Shoulder Bag" and "Satin-Finish Clutch Bag". A bag's `material` is no
`fabric` (`facet_leaves.NON_TEXTILE_TYPES`).

**Demo queries.** `black crossbody bag`, and `satin clutch bag` against the "Satin-Finish Clutch
Bag" that is faux leather; `black messenger bag`, and `leather laptop bag` against the "Faux Leather
Briefcase Laptop Bag".

**The word "belt".** About 60 belt bag titles say "belt", so the word is in 84 titles of the ceiling
build, where it was in 22, and weighs less in a text score. `mens belt 34 inch` then found a "18 Inch" fan
before any belt, because no belt's title named its audience. The webbing belt is titled "Mens Webbing
Belt", as most men's clothing is (`tests/test_retail_accessories_queries_422.py`).
