# Women's bags: crossbody, shoulder and clutch bags (gen#1114, the bag cut)

A shopper who searched for a crossbody bag, a shoulder bag or a clutch found five Handbag styles
(`bag_style` Crossbody, Shoulder or Clutch, gen#207). Each is now a type with a leaf of its own
under `Womens > Accessories > Bags`. The five Handbags stay Handbags.

| Type | Spec | Leaf | Forms (`bag_shape`) | Materials | SKU axis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crossbody Bag | `type_specs/crossbody_bag.py` | `Crossbody Bags` | Saddle, Camera, Satchel | Leather, Faux Leather, Canvas, Nylon | color x size (Small, Medium) |
| Shoulder Bag | `type_specs/shoulder_bag.py` | `Shoulder Bags` | Hobo, Baguette, Crescent | Leather, Faux Leather, Suede | color x size (Small, Medium) |
| Clutch Bag | `type_specs/clutch_bag.py` | `Clutch Bags` | Envelope, Foldover, Frame | Satin, Velvet, Faux Leather | color |

All three are derivable and offer the six main colors (gen#275). The shared pieces (the path, the
brands, the colorways, `bag_row()` and `decoy()`) are in `bags_common.py`, and the range names are
in `line_grammar_families/bags.py`. The SKUs each type holds are in
[the count table](../catalogue-mix-837.md#counts-added-by-the-gap-issues).

**What a derived style may change.** Only the material. The form, the closure and a crossbody's
strap are frozen (`scale_text.TYPE_FROZEN_KEYS`): the text and the drawing follow them, and a saddle bag
closes with a flap.

**Drawings.** `kit_bags.py` draws `crossbody_bag`, `shoulder_bag` and `clutch_bag`. `bag_shape`
picks the form and `material` the surface: a weave, ripstop lines, a nap, a sheen, a pile, a pebble
or a topstitched edge.

**Decoys.** Each type has a class 1, a class 4 and a class 6 decoy. "Crossbody Bag with Red Strap"
is a black bag whose strap is drawn red (`details.register_parts`). The red lining of the shoulder
bag and of the clutch is inside a closed bag, where no picture shows it, so only the description
names it. The class-4 decoys name a material in the title that the bag is not made of: "Faux
Leather Saddle Crossbody Bag" (`Faux Leather` is the one name, so no faux leather bag is titled
Leather-Look), "Suede-Look Shoulder Bag" and "Satin-Finish Clutch Bag". A bag's `material` is no
`fabric` (`facet_leaves.NON_TEXTILE_TYPES`).

**Demo queries.** `black crossbody bag`, and `satin clutch bag` against the "Satin-Finish Clutch
Bag" that is faux leather.
