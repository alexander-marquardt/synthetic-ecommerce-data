Refs #211

Adds eleven retail outerwear types: Trench Coat, Raincoat, Parka, Puffer Jacket, Gilet, Peacoat, Utility Jacket, Shacket, Bolero (with shrugs and capes), Windbreaker (with anoraks) and Hoodie. Main had one Coat row, a bomber, a denim jacket and a suit jacket; a search for a trench coat, a raincoat or a gilet found the one coat or a bomber. None of #30, #233 or #354 covers outerwear: this PR builds on none of them (checked by reading the issue and `gh api` on main: no `kit_outerwear.py`, no outerwear spec).

What is in it:
- `prototypes/retail_drawings/kit_outerwear.py`: eleven own shapes, each with its variant axis as a param (length, belt, breasting, hood, quilt, padding, pockets, collar, teddy, bolero style and sleeve, zip). A mark a record states is a marked group the drawing shows.
- `prototypes/retail_drawings/outerwear_rows.py` and eleven specs under `type_specs/`: four Womens styles per type and two Mens styles for Raincoat, Parka, Gilet, Utility Jacket, Windbreaker and Hoodie, three or more brands each, from clothing brands that already have a house style. Attributes are structured entries (garment_length, belted, breasting, waterproof_rating, hood, hood_type, fill, warmth_rating, packable, zip_style, fabric, ...). Each type files under Coats & Jackets.
- Decoys: every spec carries the clothing decoy plan (gen#311) with real class 2 compounds, added to `REAL_COMPOUNDS` in `tests/test_retail_apparel_decoys_311.py`; `details.py` PARTS gains a hem for each new shape.
- Puffer Jacket moves off the legacy list (`LEGACY_CEILING` 335 to 334); its old decoy plan stays in `decoy_apparel`.
- `golden-retail/stats.json` and `docs/retail-apparel-decoys.md` are regenerated with `retail_figures.py`.

Not in this PR: a Kids audience for the new types, and the "rain gauge" style cross-department incidental decoys named in the issue's WIP note (the clothing decoy plan already gives each type incidental-word decoys, class 6).
