Retail drawings: sneak peek

70 sample records in the #125 output shape. Drawings come from the parametric SVG kit (shape + fill + label + params) dressed in the brand's house style. Round 3 gives every brand a declared house style: paper, frame, outline hand and a composite logo mark, half of them as a full lockup with the name. The product's own colour and pattern never depend on the brand. Structured garment attributes are in blue. All brands, shades and fragrance names are invented.
